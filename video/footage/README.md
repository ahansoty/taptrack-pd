|---|---|---|---|
| `wrist_a.mp4` | Wearable scene, box at lower right, while the first two lines play | 16:9 landscape | ~12 s from the start |

(flips, holding still, taps, "ahhh"), ending on the result screen. Audio from these clips is not used.
