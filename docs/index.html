MHacks 2026 · Parkinson's disease

Parkinson's changes hour by hour. Neurologists see it a few times a year.

TapTrack PD is a one-minute motor check on the wrist, done several times a day and tagged with the time since the last levodopa dose. It shows the neurologist the hours they're missing: when each dose kicks in and when it wears off.

How it works

Wear it all day, dock at night, like a Holter monitor.

Step 1 of 4Meds

Log the dose

Press red when you take your medicine. Every later check knows how long it has been.

Step 2 of 4Check

Do the check

Press blue. The wrist speaks each step: hand flips, holding still, alternating taps, a steady "ahhh".

Step 3 of 4Result

Get a result

Your score against your own baseline is spoken aloud. The screen shows Good, Lower than usual, or Much lower.

Step 4 of 4Pattern

See the pattern

Two weeks of checks become a dose-response curve the neurologist can read in seconds.

What the wrist measures
  • Hand flipping: flips per second, amplitude, and how much they shrink over 10 seconds.
  • Tremor: strength while holding still, and its dominant frequency in the 3-12 Hz band.
  • Alternating taps: tap rate, rhythm regularity and slowing, on the device's own buttons.
  • Voice: loudness and steadiness of a sustained "ahhh" from the built-in microphone.
  • Passive tremor: sampled between checks whenever the wrist is still.

For clinicians

Wearing-off, visible at a glance.

Every result is tagged with time since dose. In this synthetic patient, scores peak 1 to 2.5 hours after a dose and fall about 45 points by 3 to 4 hours: the wearing-off window, measured instead of remembered. One click writes a visit report for the neurologist and a plain-language summary for the patient. Patterns only, never dose advice.

Care agent

It acts on the pattern, not just shows it.

A Fetch.ai agent on Agentverse watches the data. When a check scores red it texts the caregiver over iMessage. When a scheduled check is missed it nudges the patient. When a wearing-off pattern appears it sends the visit report to the clinic's scheduling agent and requests a follow-up. Caregivers can text back to ask how the day is going, or ask the agent in ASI:One.
